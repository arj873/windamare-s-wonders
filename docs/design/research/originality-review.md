# Originality red-team review (card R4)

Owner: research lane. Status: first pass (2026-10-29), on main `7f5704f`.

**What was read, cold:** `docs/RULES.md` (D2), `docs/design/ORIGINALITY.md` (D3),
`docs/design/CARDS.md` (D4). Because "look" is in scope, also `docs/design/ART.md`,
`src/ui/theme/tokens.css`, `public/art/**` and `docs/design/UX.md` §7-10.

**Confidence.** 7 Wonders (7W) facts below are **from memory** unless marked _confirmed_. Two
were checked by web search: Palisade is a 7W Duel card that upgrades into Fortifications
(_confirmed_); Altar is worth 2 points (_weak source_). Everything else in the 7W column is from
memory: treat it as "a 7W player will likely see this", not as a quote. All balance numbers in
section 2 are **our arithmetic, not simulated**.

Severity: **HIGH** = fix before the human approves the ruleset (H1). **MED** = should fix.
**LOW** = note it or measure it.

---

## 0. Top findings

1. **HIGH, look (art lane):** the card-type palette and type names are 7W's colour-to-role map
   one for one: raw brown, craft grey, civic blue, trade yellow, military red, science green,
   guild purple. Two of those names are blocklisted words. The resource icons include glass and
   cloth, which are 7W's crafted goods. (O1, O2)
2. **HIGH, numbers:** 9 of 14 Great Works have a 7-Renown tier and 6 of them follow
   "3 Renown, special, 7 Renown". That is the best-known 7W wonder template. (O7)
3. **HIGH, names:** `palisade` is a 7W Duel card with the same role. The Hall line
   Wayshrine → Chapel → Cathedral (2 → 4 → 7) mirrors 7W's Altar → Temple → Pantheon (2 → 3 → 7).
   (O5, O6)
4. **HIGH, design risk:** both of 7W's neighbour links were removed (no trade, PvE Raids). Our
   estimate is that well under 10% of a winning score depends on neighbours. That sets us up for
   the genre's top complaint, "multiplayer solitaire". (D1)
5. **MED, numbers bug:** with one Watch per Season, a town holds only 1 of 3 Spring Raids and
   2 of 3 Summer Raids. This contradicts CARDS.md §8's intent that one Watch per Season holds
   every Raid. (D2)

## 4. Checked and fine

Pair pick with Spend bands (closest relative: It's a Wonderful World's recycle, not 7W);
stockpiled goods with a no-Yield Winter; bank at 3 coins and a 2:1 Exchange (Catan's port, our
own lineage); Raids against a public target; Lore runs (structure differs from 7W's squares);
upgrades that cover the old card; duplicates allowed; Honours; Great Work chosen 1 of 2 and
filled by labour, never by a card; the 2-player Build/Spend/Burn variant; the tie-break; the 9
cards / 4 turns / pairs cadence; the blocklist terms in RULES.md (none found: Age, stage,
shield, science, guild and chain do not appear as mechanics).
