# How to play Windamere's Wonders

Owner: tutorial lane. A one-page summary for players. The rules are `docs/RULES.md` (rule numbers
in brackets); if the two disagree, RULES.md wins. Numbers marked † are first-pass and may change.

**The goal:** have the most **Renown** (victory points) after the Winter Raid.

You run a **town** on the Wend, the ring road round the mere of Windamere. The game has four
**Seasons**: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. About 15 minutes (untested estimate).

## Set up

- You start with **5 coins** and nothing else.
- Three **Honours** are shown to everyone. They stay up all game.
- You are dealt two **Great Works** and keep one. Everyone chooses at the same time, then all are
  revealed. Its **Perk** works from then on.
- Your **neighbours** are the towns on your left and right. They matter a lot.

## A Season

Each Season you get a hand of **6 cards**. A Season is **5 turns**.

1. Everyone picks **one card** at the same time, in secret. You can change your mind until the
   last player picks.
2. All picks are shown together and resolve.
3. You pass the rest of your hand to a neighbour. Spring and Autumn go left, Summer and Winter go
   right.

On the **fifth turn** you hold two cards. Pick one as usual. The other is your **glean**: it is
Spent for you.

## Your pick: Build or Spend

Every card can be used one of two ways.

- **Build** it into your town. Pay its cost, then it stays and works for the rest of the game.
- **Spend** it. The card is gone, and you gain what is printed on its **Spend band**: goods,
  coins or labour.

You must always pick one card. Spend is always allowed, so you are never stuck.

## Goods and costs

There are five **goods**: **reed, peat, salt, wax, horn**. They are kept in your **store** and
paid out when you Build. Left-over goods carry over, but they score nothing at the end.

If a Build needs a good you lack, you can:

- **Buy it from a neighbour.** The good leaves their store and goes straight into your Build.
  Coins go to them. The price depends on the Season: 1† coin a good in Spring, Summer and
  Autumn, 3† in Winter (which has no Yield). You can buy at most 2 goods from each neighbour a
  turn, and only what they held at the start of the turn.
- **Use an Exchange.** Some Markets let you pay 2† of one good instead of one missing good.
- **Buy from the bank** for 6† coins a good. Always the dearest way.

You cannot buy a good you already hold enough of. Nothing makes buying cheaper.

## The six kinds of card

| Kind         | What it does                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Holding**  | Gives you goods, coins or labour every turn (the **Yield**)        |
| **Hall**     | Is worth Renown. Some **upgrade** a cheaper Hall                   |
| **Market**   | Gives coins, **Exchanges**, **Tolls** and sale bonuses             |
| **Watch**    | Adds **Guard**                                                     |
| **Lore**     | Has a **rank** from 1 to 6                                         |
| **Landmark** | Scores extra at the end, for a shape of town. One of each per town |

**Yield.** After every turn's Builds, each Holding pays out. **Winter has no Yield**, so Autumn
is the time to stock up.

**Toll.** Some Markets pay you coins when a neighbour Builds.

**Upgrade.** A card that says "upgrades X" costs less if you own an X. X is then **covered**: it
stays in view but no longer counts for anything.

## Labour and your Great Work

**Labour** comes from Spend bands and some Holdings. It only goes up and is never spent. Your
Great Work has four **tiers** that complete by themselves at 4†, 9†, 15† and 22† labour. Each tier
gives a reward, usually Renown and often an effect.

## Raids

Each Season has a **Raid** with a **strength**, shown at the start of the Season. At Season end
every town compares its **Guard** with the strength.

- **Held** (Guard is enough): you gain a **Valour** token worth Renown: Spring 2†, Summer 3†,
  Autumn 4†, Winter 5†. Guard above the strength gives nothing extra.
- **Plundered** (Guard is short): you lose 1 coin for each point short, and gain no Valour.
- **Bounty:** each neighbour that held takes 1† coin from you if you were plundered.

Your Guard is never used up. It is checked again next Season.

## Lore runs

Lore cards have a rank 1 to 6. Your ranks form **runs** of consecutive numbers. A run scores by
its length: 1 → 1, 2 → 3, 3 → 6, 4 → 10, 5 → 14, 6 → 18† Renown. A second card of a rank you
already own adds nothing. Ranks 1, 2, 4, 5 score 3 + 3 = 6. A 3 joins them into a run of 5: 14.

## Honours

At the end, the town that leads each of the three Honours gains 5† Renown. A tie gives each tied
town 2†. A score of 0 never wins. The current leader of each is shown all game.

## Scoring

When the Winter Raid is done, Renown is added in six groups:

1. **Town:** Renown on your cards and Landmarks.
2. **Great Work:** completed tiers and your Perk.
3. **Raids:** your Valour tokens.
4. **Lore:** your runs.
5. **Honours:** the ones you lead.
6. **Purse:** 1 Renown for every 4† coins.

Most Renown wins. A tie goes to the most completed Great Work tiers, then the most coins.

## First game

For a table with someone new, the host can turn on **First game**: no Honours, no upgrades, and
each town is dealt one Great Work with no choice. Everything else is the same.

## Two players

Each player gets 10 cards a Season and, on turns 1 to 4, picks a second card to **Burn**: it is
thrown away unseen. The other player is your only neighbour.
